## 5. Summary / verdict

The three needed ingredients exist but are **split across three disjoint, incomplete
datasets** — none has all of {reply-region per-step hidden states, harm-delivered label +
span, injected-but-benign control}:

| dataset | cases | per-step reply hidden | judge harm label | Group-C (inj-benign) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-set clockv2 | A000–A099 ✅ | ❌ scalars only | ❌ | ✅ but scalar-only |
| Shield capture | 10 non-A prefill | ✅ [128,2,128,4096]+logits | ❌ | ❌ |
| case_XXX medical | 97×2 text | ❌ | ✅ (yes/no + rubric) | benign arm only |

**Precise `k_commit` from saved data: NO.** **Trainable `out_mask`-only detect probe from
saved data: NO** (scalars are fixed v_refusal/v_injection projections, mean-pooled over the
*whole* canvas — mixes `out_mask` with `out_unmask`, the forbidden circular read).
**→ Phase 1 dense re-capture required** (~65 harmful A-cases, Shield format extended to log:
per-step reply-region hidden at L16–L31, per-step full mask/commit trajectory, per-step
logits; run clean / harmful-injected / injected-benign arms; then judge + span).

**Blockers to flag before Phase 1:** (1) confirm primary judge (llama-vs-deepseek filename
mismatch); (2) harmful spans need locating (no stored offsets); (3) Group-C must be
*captured with hidden states*, not just scalars, to set the FPR threshold; (4) storage —
Shield format is ~257 MB/case ×65 ≈ 16 GB at L16–L31×128×128 (budget layer subset / fp16).
